FROM ONE ROOM TO THE NEXT

Moving out shouldn’t mean
throwing it all out.

The lamp you studied under. The books you’re done with. The chair that won’t fit in the car.

Someone on campus could use them. Passdown brings listings, offers, messages, and pickup plans into one place, so you can work out the handoff together.

A SMALL HANDOFF. A FRESH START.

Make room for what’s next.

THE LISTING

Give it a little spotlight.

Add a photo, describe the condition, and name your price. You can mark it free or add a pick-up-by date for move-out.

THE OFFER

Find its next person.

Review offers and accept one. The item is then reserved for that buyer, so you can focus on arranging the handoff.

THE HANDOFF

Meet. Pass it on. Done.

Use the deal’s messages to agree on a pickup place and time. Meet in person, collect payment, then mark it sold.

A good handoff starts with a good plan. Meet in a public place, inspect the item, and agree on payment together. Passdown doesn’t process payments or provide delivery.

BEFORE YOU PASS IT ON

A few good
questions.

A student project, built around the everyday stuff of campus life.

What can I find on Passdown?

Students can list used dorm gear, furniture, textbooks, electronics, kitchen essentials, and more. Browse the app to see what is actually available; the items shown on this page are illustrations.

How do I get started?

Browse available items, then sign in with Google to create a profile, list something, or make an offer. Set your school in your profile. Google sign-in doesn’t verify a person’s student status or identity.

Can I offer less or give something away?

Yes. Buyers can offer the asking price or a lower amount. Sellers decide which offer to accept. A listing can also be marked free.

How do pickup and payment work?

After an offer is accepted, use the deal’s messages and pickup plan to arrange a meeting. Check the item and pay the seller in person. Passdown doesn’t handle payments, shipping, escrow, or buyer protection.

Is this a finished commercial service?

Passdown is a CS 392 student prototype. Features may change, and availability or support isn’t guaranteed. Avoid posting sensitive information in listings or messages.

What happens to my information?

The app uses Google/Firebase for sign-in and stores profiles, listings, offers, messages, and pickup details in Firestore. Listing photos are uploaded to Cloudinary. This introduction page has no account system, forms, or analytics scripts; GitHub Pages receives ordinary hosting requests.
